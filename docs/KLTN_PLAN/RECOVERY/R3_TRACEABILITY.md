Unresolved inconsistencies requiring coordinated design closure: (1) legacy Product stock versus Ingredient recipe stock; (2) free-string units and pack Int versus base Decimal; (3) single active recipe and immutable quantities not enforced; (4) expiry boundary and reservation allocation absent; (5) GoodsReceipt/StockCount declarations without business services; (6) optional ledger IDs permit incomplete attribution, batch/actor naming differs from SQL/code; (7) Payment cardinality/status mismatch; (8) declared unique nullable keys versus absent operation replay protocol; (9) Agent JSON/fixed exports versus required registry/HITL/state machine; (10) generated15/6 versus source31/16 and unverified physical27/16. These are source observations, not deployed defects proven by PostgreSQL.

No schema, SQL, physical DB, tool permissions or business transitions changed in R3. Concrete future tool contract review: preserve current scanInventory(input,userId), recommendReorder(input,userId), createPurchaseRequestFromRecommendation(logId,userId); add any approval envelope only after D10 acceptance. Required envelope fields: action, proposal ID, canonical payload hash, source versions, actor/approver, expiry and operation key. Dispatch must reject unknown action/schema, mismatched payload, stale versions, expired/revoked approval and wrong current role before business writes. Result must identify committed operation or explicit unknown outcome. This is a proposed interface, not a new live endpoint or claimed test PASS.
